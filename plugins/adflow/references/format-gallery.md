# Format gallery: forms to pick from, static and video

The idea list of ad formats, sorted by family and checked against the rules in
[format-classification.md](format-classification.md): every entry is a form you can see, and any angle or offer fits
into it. Use it to brainstorm formats for a slot (step S2 of the [ad pipeline](ad-pipeline.md)). For tagging, the
cards in [format-classification.md](format-classification.md) decide; names here match them. Click a format for its
full card: definition, what must be true, neighbours, a quick test, examples and links to real ads.

Each family names its formats and their **variants**. A variant is a look within a format: brief it by name, but tag
it with the format, unless the brand's AI instructions in Adflow make the variant a value of its own.

Which formats fit a brand comes from the brand's format fit and its winners (see [bronnen.md](bronnen.md)), not from
this list. The column *Signal* summarises what the sources say: general tiers from broad studies, a starting point for
what to try. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides (spend first, then ROAS, per creative family). A
format that scores low in the tiers can carry one brand, and a tier-A format can fail for another.

**Sources:** Adcrate, "Ultimate guide to static ads 2026" (static specialist) · Dara Denney, "51 Meta ad creative
types" (tier list over about 20,000 ads) · the visual-formats swipe library (github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats) ·
the Adflow "UGC ad formats" inspiration list. Summarised in our own words.

## Static

### Image-led: the picture carries the ad

| Format | What you see | Variants | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Product hero**](formats/product-hero.md) | One product large in frame, short headline or none | Packshot on colour, product with one claim | Safe carrier for most brands |
| [**Lifestyle photo**](formats/lifestyle-photo.md) | Product in use or in a real setting | Instagram text overlay (native post look with short text) | Text overlay is the largest group in the swipe library |
| [**Flat lay**](formats/flat-lay.md) | Products laid out, shot from above | What's in the box | Works for sets and multi-part products |
| [**Grid**](formats/grid.md) | Several products or photos in separate tiles | Bento grid, mood board, collection | Both sources positive (tier A as grid static) |
| [**Split screen**](formats/split-screen.md) | The image split into two halves side by side | Us vs them in two pictures | Strong for contrast |
| [**Before / after**](formats/before-after.md) | Same subject in two states | Toggle (on/off switch), progression, timeline | Both sources positive for transformation products |
| [**Ad in the wild**](formats/ad-in-the-wild.md) | The ad shown in a real place: billboard, poster, phone mockup | AI billboard | Stops the scroll; tier D as AI billboard |
| [**Sign**](formats/sign.md) | A person or place holding a sign with the message | Written on body | Physical, native feel |

### Text-led: the text in a fixed layout carries the ad

| Format | What you see | Variants | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Headline + benefits**](formats/headline-benefits.md) | Big headline with two to four benefits | Callout list | Adcrate's number one; tier B as headline |
| [**Feature callouts**](formats/feature-callouts.md) | Lines or arrows from the product to labelled features | Pointout | Good for products with visible features |
| [**Comparison table**](formats/comparison-table.md) | The product next to an alternative in rows or columns | Us vs them table, price per use | Both sources positive |
| [**Listicle**](formats/listicle.md) | A numbered or bulleted list is the main element | "3 reasons", checklist | Tier E as static |
| [**Statistic**](formats/statistic.md) | One big number as the eye-catcher | Line chart, case study result | Tier C; needs a strong, true number |
| [**Diagram**](formats/diagram.md) | A chart that explains a choice | Flowchart, venn diagram, matching chart (which one suits you) | New in the swipe library; good for ranges with several products |
| [**Letter**](formats/letter.md) | A personal letter or note, signed by a person | Founder's letter, handwritten card | Tier B as founder's letter |
| [**Post-it**](formats/post-it.md) | The message as a handwritten sticky note | Notes on a fridge | Adcrate positive, tier F as notes app: depends on the brand |
| [**Announcement**](formats/announcement.md) | Looks like a notice, flyer or policy | Flyer, "new rule" or gimmick look | Adcrate: gimmicky but often works |
| [**Press**](formats/press.md) | Looks like an article or shows press logos | Breaking news, advertorial | Adcrate positive, tier F: depends on the brand |
| [**Meme**](formats/meme.md) | A recognisable meme template | | Broad, younger audiences |

### Native screenshot: looks like a phone screen

| Format | What you see | Variants | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Comment screenshot**](formats/comment-screenshot.md) | A real social comment or review as a screenshot | Several comments stacked | Social proof that looks organic |
| [**Comment response**](formats/comment-response.md) | A comment plus the brand's reply as a screenshot | | Good for objections and FAQs |
| [**Text message**](formats/text-message.md) | A chat or message thread | WhatsApp, iMessage | Native feel |
| [**Notes app**](formats/notes-app.md) | A phone note | Shopping list, to-do list | Tier F, but native |
| [**Web search**](formats/web-search.md) | Search results or a search bar | Autocomplete | Curiosity-led |
| [**Receipt**](formats/receipt.md) | An itemised receipt, ticket, coupon or cart screen is the hero | Paper receipt, coupon, cart screenshot | Few examples; used around sales and bundles |
| [**Quote card**](formats/quote-card.md) | A customer quote designed as the main visual | Star rating card, review grid | Adcrate favourite, tier E: depends on the brand |

## Video

### A person to camera

| Format | What you see | Variants | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Talking head**](formats/talking-head.md) | One person speaks to camera for most of the video | Yapper (fast, raw), walk and talk, Q&A with an off-screen interviewer | Tier A as yapper |
| [**Street interview**](formats/street-interview.md) | People on the street answer or try the product | Blind test with strangers | Tier C |
| [**Podcast**](formats/podcast.md) | Two or more people at microphones, podcast set | Podcast clip | Tier F |
| [**Green screen**](formats/green-screen.md) | A person in front of a screenshot, page or video they react to | Reaction to a viral video, stitch | Tier A as green screen commentary |
| [**Comment reaction**](formats/comment-reaction.md) | A comment on screen at the start, then the answer | | Tier B; good for objections |
| [**Whiteboard**](formats/whiteboard.md) | A person explains on a whiteboard or flip chart | | Good for a mechanism |
| [**Phone call**](formats/phone-call.md) | A recorded call, phone on screen | Calling customers | Rare, feels real |

### The product on screen

| Format | What you see | Variants | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Product demo**](formats/product-demo.md) | The product used, poured, applied or worn | How-to, stress test, multitasking demo | The classic; leaning only on it causes fatigue |
| [**Unboxing**](formats/unboxing.md) | A package opened and the product taken out | | Strong for gifts and sets |
| [**Try-on**](formats/try-on.md) | A person puts the product on and shows how it looks | Styling, stacking | Strong for fashion and jewellery |
| [**First-try reaction**](formats/first-try-reaction.md) | Someone tries the product for the first time and reacts | Taste test, blind test | Strong against "is it any good" doubts |
| [**Before / after**](formats/before-after.md) | The same subject in two states | Progression | Tier B |

### Footage with voice or text

| Format | What you see | Variants | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**B-roll with voice-over**](formats/b-roll-with-voice-over.md) | Mixed footage, a voice tells the story, no speaker on screen | Photo slideshow with voice-over | Common; plain problem-solution in this form is tier C |
| [**B-roll with text**](formats/b-roll-with-text.md) | Footage, text on screen carries the story, no speech | Text-only | Tier E as text-only |
| [**Behind the scenes**](formats/behind-the-scenes.md) | Making, packing or the workplace | Warehouse, founder at work | Tier C as warehouse |
| [**POV**](formats/pov.md) | First-person camera, hands in frame | | Native feel |
| [**Vlog**](formats/vlog.md) | Handheld, following someone through the day | Day in the life, get ready with me, staged moment | Tier D: scales poorly on paid |
| [**Skit**](formats/skit.md) | An acted scene with roles | Talking back and forth (one person, two roles), clone | Strong for relatable problems |
| [**Animation**](formats/animation.md) | Drawn, motion graphics or claymation | AI animation | Tier C |

## Ideas from the sources that are not a format

These appear in the source lists as "formats" but fail the rules. Put them where they belong and pick a form from
the gallery for them.

| Source idea | What it is | Where it goes | Typical forms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Testimonial, review ad | Message | Angle: social proof | Quote card, comment screenshot, talking head, first-try reaction |
| Offer | Message | Offer | Product hero, headline + benefits |
| Persona-based | Audience | Persona | Any |
| Founders ad | Sender | Creator: founder | Talking head, letter, behind the scenes |
| Celebrity, authority, expert, EGC | Sender | Creator | Talking head |
| UGC, raw UGC, ugly ads | Production | Tag | Talking head, product demo, try-on |
| AI generated, AI avatar | Production | Tag | Any |
| Partnership ad | Placement | Launch setting | Any |
| Problem-solution, educational, personal story, VSL | Structure | A structure dimension if the brand wants one | B-roll with voice-over, talking head |
| Comparison "this vs that" | Message | Angle | Comparison table, split screen |
| David and Goliath, challenging beliefs, amateur investigation | Message | Angle | Talking head, green screen |
| Challenge | Concept | Angle or campaign | Talking head, product demo |
| Actionable step plus dream result | Hook | Hook | Any |
| Setting change, shot change, multitasking | Editing technique | Brief | Talking head, product demo |
| Catalog, carousel | Ad type | Ad type in Adflow | Product hero, grid |
