# Ad in the wild

The ad shown as if it lives in a real place: on a billboard, a poster, a bus shelter or a phone screen in a hand.

Family: Static, image-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A real or realistic surrounding is visible around the ad: a street, a station, a hand holding a phone.
- The message sits on a placement surface (billboard, poster, screen mockup), not straight on the photo.
- The whole reads as a photo of an ad spotted in the world.

## Not to confuse with

- **Lifestyle photo:** the product is in a scene, but there is no ad surface in it.
- **Announcement:** a homemade flyer or notice taped up; the look of a notice wins.
- **Sign:** a person holds up a handwritten sign.

## Quick test

Is there an ad inside the picture, placed in a real-looking scene?

## Does not belong in the format

- Made with AI or a mockup template: production, a tag.
- "Now in stores" or "launch": Angle.

## Variants

- **AI billboard:** an oversized or impossible billboard built with AI. Tag it as Ad in the wild.

## Examples

- ✅ A huge billboard over a motorway reading "Your back called. It wants a better chair."
- ✅ A phone held on a train, showing the brand's story with a three-step skincare routine.
- ❌ A man in a park holding a cardboard sign with the same line: Sign.
