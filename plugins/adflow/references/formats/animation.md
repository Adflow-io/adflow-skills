# Animation

The video is drawn, motion graphics, rendered or claymation instead of filmed live action.

Family: Video, footage with voice or text.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The picture is animated for most of the video: products, text, shapes or characters move.
- No filmed live-action person or scene carries it; real objects moved frame by frame (stop motion) count.

## Not to confuse with

- **B-roll with text:** filmed footage with text, even when the text moves.
- **Whiteboard:** marker on a real board, filmed.

## Quick test

Is most of what you see drawn, rendered or moved frame by frame, not filmed live? Check what is on screen in the
per-second analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- AI generated: production, a tag. AI animation is Animation because the result is animated; an AI video that looks
  live-action is not.
- An animated offer banner: the offer is Offer; the form is still Animation.

## Variants

- **Motion graphics:** products and text slide, pop and cycle. Tag it as Animation.
- **AI animation:** AI-generated animated characters or a mascot. Tag it as Animation.
- **Stop motion or claymation:** objects or clay moved frame by frame. Tag it as Animation.
- **Doodle:** an original hand-drawn character or scene, lightly animated. Tag it as Animation.

## Examples

- ✅ Three cereal boxes slide in, flip and line up while the price ticks down.
- ✅ A cartoon tooth with a face explains why it hates sugar, ending on a toothpaste tube.
- ❌ Filmed shots of the cereal boxes on a table with moving text: B-roll with text.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=product-animation` (22 videos, many also tagged
  `offer-banner`), `vf=ai-animation` (2), `vf=stop-motion` (1) and `vf=doodle` (1 video), and its notes in
  [formats/product-animation](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/product-animation), [formats/ai-animation](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/ai-animation),
  [formats/stop-motion](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/stop-motion) and [formats/doodle](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/doodle).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.
