# Announcement

The ad looks like a notice rather than an ad: a flyer, an official announcement, a new rule or a policy.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The layout borrows from a notice: a flyer, memo, policy, warning label or "attention" sign.
- The heading reads like a notice, not like a slogan.
- Often a printed-sheet look (taped up, torn tabs, stamps) or official styling.

## Not to confuse with

- **Headline + benefits:** a normal ad layout, not dressed up as a notice.
- **Ad in the wild:** a paid placement such as a billboard or transit poster.
- **Letter:** personal and signed.
- **Press:** looks like media coverage.

## Quick test

At first glance, would you take it for a notice, flyer or rule instead of an ad?

## Does not belong in the format

- A launch or "new product": Angle. "Sale ends Sunday": Offer.

## Variants

- **Flyer:** a printed sheet taped up in a real place, such as a lamppost or notice board. Tag it as Announcement.
- **"New rule" or gimmick look:** a mock policy, memo or warning label. Tag it as Announcement.

## Examples

- ✅ A torn-tab flyer on a community board: "Lost: my afternoon slump. Found: this tea."
- ✅ A mock office memo, "New policy: no more sad desk lunches", for a meal-prep box.
- ❌ The same line on a giant billboard by a motorway: Ad in the wild.
