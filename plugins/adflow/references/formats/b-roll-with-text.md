# B-roll with text

Footage plays with text on screen carrying the story, and nobody speaks.

Family: Video, footage with voice or text.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- There is no speech: no voice-over and no one talking to camera. Music is fine.
- Text on screen carries the message.
- The picture is filmed footage or plain backgrounds, not animation.

## Not to confuse with

- **B-roll with voice-over:** a spoken voice carries the story.
- **Animation:** the picture is drawn, motion graphics or rendered.
- **Product demo:** one sustained use of the product carries the video, the text only labels it. A text card only at
  the start, then the use carries the rest: Product demo.
- **Product hero (static):** a short video that is a static in motion (product placed or moving, big text carries the
  message) is still a video: tag it B-roll with text.

## Quick test

With the sound on, does anyone speak? If not, and text carries the message, it is this card. Use the per-second
analysis in Adflow: song lyrics are not speech. Never judge from the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- The offer in big type over the footage: Offer. Listicle wording, "3 reasons": structure.
- Native or edited text style: production.

## Variants

- **Text-only:** text on a plain or moving background, almost no footage. Tag it as B-roll with text.
- **Native story text:** the platform's own story text over footage. Tag it as B-roll with text.

## Examples

- ✅ Slow shots of a candle being lit, with three lines of text about falling asleep faster.
- ✅ Clips of a dog on a walk, text telling how the harness stopped the pulling.
- ❌ The same dog clips with the owner's voice telling the story: B-roll with voice-over.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=b-roll-overlay` (12 videos) and `vf=instagram-text-overlay`
  (4 videos; the slug is shared with statics), and its notes in [formats/b-roll-overlay](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/b-roll-overlay)
  and [formats/instagram-text-overlay](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/instagram-text-overlay).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.
