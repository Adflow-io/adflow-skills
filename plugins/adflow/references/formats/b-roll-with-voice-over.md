# B-roll with voice-over

Mixed footage is cut together while a voice off screen tells the story.

Family: Video, footage with voice or text.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The picture is mixed footage: product, people, places, details, cut together.
- A spoken voice-over carries the story; the speaker is not on screen for most of the video.

## Not to confuse with

- **B-roll with text:** no one speaks; text on screen carries the story.
- **Behind the scenes:** the footage shows making, packing or the workplace.
- **Product demo:** one sustained use of the product, not mixed footage. Shots of pouring or using the product inside
  mixed footage under a voice stay B-roll with voice-over.
- **Talking head:** the speaker is on screen for most of the video.

## Quick test

Is there speech for most of the video, with the speaker off screen? Check speech versus song lyrics in the per-second
analysis in Adflow; lyrics are not a voice-over. Never judge from the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Problem-solution, personal story, VSL, transformation: structure. The library's `transformation` is not a card.
- AI voice, a dense gag-heavy edit: production or editing. The library's `high-production-edit` is not a card.

## Variants

- **Photo slideshow with voice-over:** stills instead of moving footage. Tag it as B-roll with voice-over.

## Examples

- ✅ Shots of a rainy commute, a soaked shoe and a dry pair of socks, while a woman's voice tells how the jacket held up.
- ✅ Family photos fade in and out while a father's voice explains why he started using a photo-book app.
- ❌ The same commute shots with only text and music: B-roll with text.
- Real ads: No examples in the library yet.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

Dara Denney's tier list finds it common and puts plain problem-solution in this form at tier C. Not a rule for
every brand.
