# Before / after

The same subject shown in two states, before and after: two images side by side in a static, or one after the other
in a video.

Family: Static, image-led. Also a video format (Video, product on screen); this one card covers both.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The same subject appears twice: the same face, floor, garden, room or drawer.
- The two states are marked or obvious: labels, dates, a switch, or a cut between matched shots in video.
- The change between the two is the centrepiece of the ad.

## Not to confuse with

- **Split screen:** two halves showing different subjects.
- **Product demo (video):** the product is used on screen, without a matched before and after shot.
- **Statistic:** a number is the proof, not a pair of images.

## Quick test

Is it the same subject twice, in two states?

## Does not belong in the format

- "Results" or "transformation" as the message: Angle. A time claim ("after 4 weeks") is copy.
- A problem-to-result story told in sequence without matched shots (the library's `transformation`): structure. Pick
  the format from what is on screen, for example B-roll with voice-over or Product demo.

## Variants

- **Toggle:** a settings-style on/off switch over the image marks the two states. Tag it as Before / after.
- **Progression or timeline:** more than two states in a row (week 1, 4, 8). Tag it as Before / after.

## Examples

- ✅ Static: a patio, left half green with moss, right half clean after the spray, labelled before and after.
- ✅ Video: a messy kitchen drawer, cut to the same drawer sorted with the dividers, for an organising angle.
- ❌ A cluttered drawer next to a different, styled drawer from the brand's catalogue: Split screen.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, `vf=before-and-after` (9 examples: 6 static, 3 video) and `vf=toggle` (1); notes:
  [formats/before-and-after](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/before-and-after), [formats/toggle](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/toggle).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.
