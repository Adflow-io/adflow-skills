# Behind the scenes

Footage of how the product is made, packed or tested, or of the place where that happens.

Family: Video, footage with voice or text.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The footage shows making, developing, testing, packing or shipping the product, or the workplace itself.
- A voice-over or text carries it; a person may appear at work but does not talk to camera for most of it.

## Not to confuse with

- **Product demo:** the finished product in use, not its making.
- **B-roll with voice-over:** mixed footage of life and use, not of production.
- **Talking head:** the founder talks to camera for most of the video, even when standing in the warehouse.

## Quick test

Is what you see mostly the making, packing or workplace behind the product? Check what is on screen in the per-second
analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Founder, employee, maker: Creator. The library's `founder` and `egc` labels are not cards.
- Craft, quality, "made by hand", small business: the message, Angle.

## Variants

- **Warehouse or packing:** orders picked, packed and shipped. Tag it as Behind the scenes.
- **Founder at work:** the founder making or testing, voice-over on top. Tag it as Behind the scenes.
- **Lab or test:** recipes, samples and tests during development. Tag it as Behind the scenes.

## Examples

- ✅ Hands pour soy wax into jars in a small workshop while text explains why each candle cures for two weeks.
- ✅ A packing table fills with orders on a Monday morning, the owner's voice counting how many went out.
- ❌ The owner talking to camera in the same workshop for the whole video: Talking head.
