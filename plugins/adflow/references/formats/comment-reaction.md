# Comment reaction

A viewer's comment or question is on screen at the start, and the rest of the video answers it.

Family: Video, person to camera.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- In the first seconds a comment or question is visible as text: a reply bubble, comment card or question sticker.
- The rest of the video is the answer: a person to camera, a demo or footage with text.
- The comment stays the anchor: the video only makes sense as a reply to it.

## Not to confuse with

- **Talking head:** no comment on screen. "Someone asked me…" said aloud, without the comment shown, is a Talking head.
- **Green screen:** the background is a page, article or video, not a comment being answered.
- **Comment response (static):** a still screenshot of a comment plus the brand's reply; this card is for video.

## Quick test

Is a comment or question readable on screen in the first seconds, and is the video an answer to it? Check the text
on screen in the per-second analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Objection handling, FAQ, price question: the message, Angle.
- Whether the comment is real or written for the ad, and who answers (founder, creator): production and Creator.

## Variants

- **Reply bubble or comment card:** the platform's own comment look. Tag it as Comment reaction.
- **Question sticker:** the "ask me a question" box from stories. Tag it as Comment reaction.
- **Answered by demo:** the reply is shown with the product instead of spoken. Tag it as Comment reaction.

## Examples

- ✅ A reply bubble asks "does this pan really not stick?"; a cook cracks an egg into it without oil.
- ✅ A question sticker asks "is it worth the price?"; the founder of a shoe brand answers to camera.
- ❌ The same founder answering the price question without any comment on screen: Talking head.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=comment-response` (15 videos; the slug is shared with
  statics), and its notes in [formats/comment-response](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/comment-response).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

Dara Denney's tier list puts it in tier B; the gallery flags it as good for objections. Not a rule for every
brand.
