# Comment response

A comment or question from a user plus the brand's answer, both shown in the look of a social app.

Family: Static, native screenshot.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A comment or question is visible as app UI: a comment with avatar, or a story question sticker.
- The answer is visible too: a reply under the comment, or text written over the photo.
- Both read as a native screen, not a designed quote.

## Not to confuse with

- **Comment screenshot:** the comment alone, no reply.
- **Text message:** a private chat thread.
- **Comment reaction (video):** a comment on screen, then a person answers it on camera.

## Quick test

Do you see both a comment and the brand's answer to it?

## Does not belong in the format

- The objection or FAQ being answered: Angle.
- Who answers (founder, support, a creator): Creator.

## Variants

- **Question sticker:** the question sits in a story question box; the answer is written over the photo. Tag it as
  Comment response.

## Examples

- ✅ A comment "does it work on curly hair?" and the brand's reply "yes, here's how" over a photo of curls.
- ✅ A question box "is the strap vegan?" answered in story text over a close-up of the watch.
- ❌ The comment alone over the photo, no reply: Comment screenshot.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=comment-response` (26 examples: 11 static, 15 video; built on
  the question sticker), and its notes in
  [formats/comment-response](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/comment-response).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

Good for objections and FAQs (gallery); one of the largest groups in the swipe library.
