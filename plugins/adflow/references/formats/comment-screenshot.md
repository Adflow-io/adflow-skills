# Comment screenshot

A social comment or review shown as a screenshot, with the app's own look, and no reply from the brand.

Family: Static, native screenshot.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- Comment UI is visible: avatar, username, comment text, likes, "reply" or a timestamp.
- One comment, or several stacked, is the hero, often over a product or photo.
- No reply from the brand is shown.

## Not to confuse with

- **Quote card:** a designed quote without app chrome.
- **Comment response:** the comment plus the brand's reply.
- **Text message:** chat bubbles between two people.

## Quick test

Does it look like a screenshot of someone's comment under a post, with nobody answering?

## Does not belong in the format

- Social proof or a review as the message: Angle.
- The swipe library counts comments with a reply here too; under our rules a reply makes it Comment response.

## Variants

- **Several comments stacked:** two to four comments one above the other. Tag it as Comment screenshot.

## Examples

- ✅ One comment, "ok who else bought this after the third video", over a photo of the blender.
- ✅ Three stacked comments about fast delivery over a packshot, for a shipping objection.
- ❌ The same comment with the brand's reply under it: Comment response.
