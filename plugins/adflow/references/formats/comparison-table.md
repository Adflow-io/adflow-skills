# Comparison table

The product next to an alternative in a table of rows and columns, usually with ticks and crosses.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- Two or more columns (or rows): the product and at least one alternative.
- Several rows of criteria, each marked per column: ticks and crosses, numbers or prices.
- The table is the main element; images are small or in the column headers.

## Not to confuse with

- **Split screen:** two pictures side by side, no rows.
- **Diagram:** a matching chart maps your situation to a product, not product against alternative.
- **Listicle:** one list, no second column.

## Quick test

Is there a table where the product and an alternative are compared row by row?

## Does not belong in the format

- "Us vs them" and who the "them" is (competitor, category, the old way): Angle.
- A lower price as the argument: Angle or Offer; the table only shows it.

## Variants

- **Us vs them table:** the brand's column all ticks, the alternative's all crosses. Tag it as Comparison table.
- **Price per use:** rows compare the cost per use or per serving. Tag it as Comparison table.

## Examples

- ✅ "Our toothpaste tablets" against "tube toothpaste", five rows: plastic-free, travel-safe, no mess, and so on.
- ✅ Cost per cup: coffee pods, a café latte and the brand's cold brew concentrate, for a saving angle.
- ❌ A photo of a plastic tube on the left, the tablets in a tin on the right, no rows: Split screen.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=us-vs-them` (22 examples), and its notes in
  [formats/us-vs-them](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/us-vs-them).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.
