# Diagram

A chart that helps the viewer choose or shows where the product fits: a flowchart, a venn diagram or a matching chart.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A visible chart structure: arrows with forks, overlapping circles, or a lookup table ("you are X, pick Y").
- The chart routes the viewer to an answer, or places the product where needs meet.
- The chart is the main element; images are small or part of the answers.

## Not to confuse with

- **Comparison table:** the product against an alternative, row by row.
- **Statistic:** a number or a line chart shows a result; nothing to choose.
- **Feature callouts:** labels point at parts of one product.

## Quick test

Do you follow the chart to find which option suits you, or where the product sits?

## Does not belong in the format

- "For every skin type" or a quiz idea: Angle or Persona. A quiz landing page: landing page, not the ad.

## Variants

- **Flowchart:** yes/no forks with arrows that end on a product. Tag it as Diagram.
- **Venn diagram:** overlapping circles, the product in the overlap. Tag it as Diagram.
- **Matching chart:** your type in the rows, read across to your product. Tag it as Diagram.

## Examples

- ✅ "Which roast is yours?": "Milk in your coffee?" yes or no, arrows to three bags of coffee.
- ✅ Three circles, "tastes good", "no added sugar", "keeps you full", with the snack bar in the middle.
- ❌ A line rising over eight weeks to show hydration: Statistic (line chart).
