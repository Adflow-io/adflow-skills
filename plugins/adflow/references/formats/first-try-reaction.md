# First-try reaction

Someone tries the product for the first time on camera and their reaction is the point.

Family: Video, product on screen.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A person tastes, smells or uses the product on camera, presented as the first time.
- Their reaction is visible and heard: face, words, a verdict.
- The trying and the reaction fill the core of the video, not one beat in a longer pitch.

## Not to confuse with

- **Street interview:** strangers stopped in public who try it.
- **Talking head:** the person talks about the product but does not try it on camera.
- **Product demo:** the product is used to show what it does, without a first reaction as the point.

## Quick test

Is the moment of trying, and the reaction to it, the reason the video exists? Check the action and the speech in the
per-second analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Selfie, UGC, filmed by a partner: production, a tag.
- "Better than expected", "is it any good": the message, Angle. Who tries it: Creator.

## Variants

- **Taste test:** the product is eaten or drunk and the taste judged. Tag it as First-try reaction.
- **Blind test:** the person does not know which product is which until the reveal. Tag it as First-try reaction.
- **Ranking:** several flavours or versions tried in a row and ranked. Tag it as First-try reaction.

## Examples

- ✅ A man sips an alcohol-free gin and tonic for the first time and pulls a surprised face.
- ✅ A mother tries an unscented baby lotion and says out loud what she notices.
- ❌ The same man explaining the gin's botanicals without drinking it: Talking head.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=taste-test` (1 video, also tagged yapper), and its notes in
  [formats/taste-test](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/taste-test).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

The gallery (from its sources) rates it strong against "is it any good" doubts. Not a rule for every brand.
