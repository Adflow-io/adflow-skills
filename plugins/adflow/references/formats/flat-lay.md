# Flat lay

Products and props laid out on one surface and photographed from directly above.

Family: Static, image-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The camera looks straight down (top-down).
- It is one photo of one real surface, not separate tiles or cut-outs.
- The items are arranged on purpose; the arrangement shows the range, the set or the contents.

## Not to confuse with

- **Grid:** separate tiles or cut-outs placed by design, not one photo.
- **Lifestyle photo:** a scene shot from the side, the product in use.
- **Product hero:** one product, front view, plain background.

## Quick test

Is it one top-down photo of things laid out on a surface?

## Does not belong in the format

- "Bundle", "gift set" or "routine": the message (Angle) or the deal (Offer).
- Styled studio shoot versus a phone photo on the kitchen table: production, a tag.

## Variants

- **What's in the box:** the opened package with each item laid out next to it. Tag it as Flat lay.

## Examples

- ✅ Five skincare bottles on marble seen from above, each with a small step number.
- ✅ A camping cook set unpacked on a wooden table from above, with "Everything fits in one pot".
- ❌ The same five bottles as cut-outs in a 2×3 tile layout: Grid.
