# Green screen

A person appears in front of a full-frame screenshot, page, chart or video and talks about what is on it.

Family: Video, person to camera.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A full-frame screen fills the background: an article, product page, review, chart, photo or video.
- The person is in front of it from the first seconds; what they say relates to what is on it.

## Not to confuse with

- **Talking head:** no screen behind the person, or graphics only pop up beside them in a real room (graphic yap).
- **Comment reaction:** the opening element is a comment or question that the video answers, even when it fills the
  background.

## Quick test

In the first seconds, is a person in front of a full-frame screen they talk about? Check what is on screen in the
per-second analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Who speaks (founder, expert, creator): Creator.
- Debunking, "I looked into it", David and Goliath, a press mention: the message, Angle.
- How it was composited (app effect, edit): production.

## Variants

- **Reaction to a viral video, or stitch:** the background is someone else's video. Tag it as Green screen.
- **Point to screen:** a phone films a real laptop or phone screen and a hand points through it while a voice talks.
  Tag it as Green screen.

## Examples

- ✅ A creator in front of a news article about microplastics explains why she switched water filters.
- ✅ A man in front of a product page scrolls through the reviews and reacts to the price.
- ❌ The same man in his living room talking to camera, no screen behind him: Talking head.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=greenscreen` (9 videos) and `vf=point-to-screen` (1), and its
  notes in [formats/greenscreen](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/greenscreen) and [formats/point-to-screen](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/point-to-screen).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.
