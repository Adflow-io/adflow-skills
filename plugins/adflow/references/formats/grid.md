# Grid

Several products or photos in separate tiles or cut-outs, arranged together on one canvas.

Family: Static, image-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- Three or more separate images, each in its own tile, cell or cut-out.
- No single photo holds them all; you can see where one image ends and the next starts.
- The set of images together is the payoff: the range, the collection, the mix.

## Not to confuse with

- **Flat lay:** one overhead photo of a real surface.
- **Split screen:** exactly two halves, side by side.
- **Before / after:** two images of the same subject in two states.

## Quick test

Do you see several separate images instead of one photo?

## Does not belong in the format

- "Collection launch" or "bundle": Angle or Offer. A catalogue or carousel: the ad type in Adflow.

## Variants

- **Product grid:** only packshots or cut-outs, in cells or rows. Tag it as Grid.
- **Bento grid:** tiles of mixed types, at least one lifestyle, in-use, person or close-up tile. Tag it as Grid.
- **Mood board or collage:** loose cut-outs pasted on one canvas, overlapping, no cells. Tag it as Grid.
