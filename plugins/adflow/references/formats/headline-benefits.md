# Headline + benefits

A big headline with two to four short benefits under or around it, usually next to the product.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- One large headline is the first thing you read.
- Two to four short benefit lines, often with icons, ticks or pills.
- The benefits are about the whole product; nothing points at a specific part of it.

## Not to confuse with

- **Feature callouts:** lines or arrows run from each label to a part of the product.
- **Listicle:** the list itself is the main element, often numbered and longer.
- **Product hero:** a packshot with one short line, no benefit block.
- **Announcement:** dressed up as a notice, flyer or policy.

## Quick test

Could you move the benefit lines anywhere on the image and they still make sense? Then it is this format.

## Does not belong in the format

- A discount in the headline: Offer. A bold or funny tone: copy style.
- Which benefits you pick: Angle.

## Variants

- **Callout list:** benefits as icon rows or tick pills around the product, without arrows. Tag it as
  Headline + benefits.

## Examples

- ✅ "Sleep through the night" above a pillow, with three ticks: cooling cover, washable, 100-night trial.
- ✅ "The lunchbox that doesn't leak" with three icon rows for a parent who packs lunches daily.
- ❌ The same pillow with arrows to the cover, the filling and the zip, each labelled: Feature callouts.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=feature-benefit-callout` (4 examples, our callout list), and
  its notes in [formats/feature-benefit-callout](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/feature-benefit-callout).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.
