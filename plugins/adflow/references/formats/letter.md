# Letter

A personal letter or note fills the ad: one or more paragraphs under a short heading, signed by a person.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A block of text (one or more paragraphs) is the main element.
- It looks like a letter, card or note on paper or a plain page, typed or handwritten.
- A salutation, a signature or a name closes it.

## Not to confuse with

- **Notes app:** the text sits in a phone note with its app bar.
- **Post-it:** one short line on a sticky note.
- **Announcement:** a notice, policy or flyer, not a personal note.
- **Press:** the layout of a publication.

## Quick test

Is it a block of text that reads as a note from one person, signed?

## Does not belong in the format

- Who signs it (founder, CEO, team): Creator.
- An apology, a story or a thank-you: Angle.

## Variants

- **Founder's letter:** a typed letter on paper or a plain page. Tag it as Letter; the founder goes in Creator.
- **Handwritten card:** a notecard written by hand. Tag it as Letter.

## Examples

- ✅ A typed letter on cream paper, "To everyone who waited for the restock", signed by the head baker.
- ✅ A handwritten card from the packing team explaining why the box has no plastic.
- ❌ Two handwritten words on a yellow sticky note on the box: Post-it.
