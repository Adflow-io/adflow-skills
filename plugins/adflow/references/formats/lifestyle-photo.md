# Lifestyle photo

One photo of the product in use or in a real setting, with or without a person, and at most a short text on top.

Family: Static, image-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A real setting is visible: a room, a street, nature, a body, a hand.
- The product is in that scene, in use or placed there.
- It is one photo, not tiles; any text sits on the photo and does not fill it.

## Not to confuse with

- **Product hero:** plain or studio background, no scene.
- **Flat lay:** a top-down arrangement of items, not a scene.
- **Ad in the wild:** the scene holds a placed ad (billboard, poster, phone mockup).

## Quick test

Is the product in a real place or in someone's hands, in one photo?

## Does not belong in the format

- The person in the photo (creator, founder, customer): Creator.
- "UGC look", "selfie" or "shot on a phone": production, a tag. An answered question sticker makes it Comment response.

## Variants

- **Instagram text overlay:** looks like someone's own story or post, with the app's native text, link or image
  stickers. Tag it as Lifestyle photo.
- **Shelfie:** products standing upright on a styled shelf at home. Tag it as Lifestyle photo.

## Examples

- ✅ A mirror selfie at the gym holding a water bottle, story-style text "day 12 of 2 litres a day".
- ✅ A candle burning on a bedside table at night, no text, for a better-sleep angle.
- ❌ The same candle cut out on a plain beige background: Product hero.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, `vf=instagram-text-overlay` (49 examples) and `vf=shelfie` (5), notes in
  [formats/instagram-text-overlay](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/instagram-text-overlay), [formats/shelfie](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/shelfie).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

The Instagram text overlay is the largest static group in the visual-formats swipe library; whether it beats a
packshot differs per brand.
