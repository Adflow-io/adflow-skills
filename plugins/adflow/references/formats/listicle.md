# Listicle

A numbered or bulleted list is the main element of the ad, in a plain designed layout.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A list of separate items, numbered or with bullets or ticks, takes most of the space.
- You read it item by item; a short title introduces it.
- The list sits in a designed layout, not inside a phone note, sticky note or letter.

## Not to confuse with

- **Headline + benefits:** the headline carries the ad and two to four benefits support it.
- **Notes app:** the list is typed in a phone note with its app bar; the native screenshot wins.
- **Post-it:** the list is handwritten on a sticky note.

## Quick test

Is the list itself the thing you read, in a layout that is not a phone note, sticky note or letter?

## Does not belong in the format

- "Reasons to buy" or "mistakes to avoid": Angle. A listicle script in video: structure.
- The swipe library tags a list together with its carrier (notes app, post-it); we pick one box, the carrier wins.

## Variants

- **"3 reasons":** numbered reasons under a title. Tag it as Listicle.
- **Checklist:** ticked items, often ending on the product. Tag it as Listicle.

## Examples

- ✅ "5 things nobody tells you about running shoes" over a shoe photo, five numbered lines.
- ✅ A checklist "Packed for the weekend?" with ticked items, the last one the travel kit.
- ❌ The same five lines typed in a phone note with the app bar visible: Notes app.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=listicle` (5 examples: 2 static, 3 video), and its notes in
