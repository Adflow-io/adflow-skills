# Meme

The ad is built on a recognisable meme template or meme style, with the product slotted into the joke.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A recognisable meme layout: an image macro, a two-panel reaction, "expectation vs reality", stick figures.
- Top or panel captions in the meme's own style.
- It reads as shareable content, not as an ad layout.

## Not to confuse with

- **Lifestyle photo:** a plain photo with text, no meme template.
- **Before / after:** the same subject in two real states as proof, not a joke template.
- **Split screen:** two halves without a meme layout.

## Quick test

Would someone share it as a meme without the logo?

## Does not belong in the format

- Humour as the tone: copy style. A trend or a relatable pain: Angle.

## Variants

- None named yet. Tag every meme template as Meme.

## Examples

- ✅ A two-panel reaction: turning away from "ironing a shirt", pointing happily at "the wrinkle spray".
- ✅ A stick-figure comic, "me at 7am" against "me after the first sip", with the coffee tin in the corner.
- ❌ A photo of a friend laughing with the product and a short caption: Lifestyle photo.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=meme` (2 examples), and its notes in
