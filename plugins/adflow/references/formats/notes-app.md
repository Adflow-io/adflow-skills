# Notes app

A screenshot of a phone note, with the app's bar and typed text carrying the message.

Family: Static, native screenshot.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The chrome of a phone note app is visible: back arrow, share or done button, toolbar.
- Typed text in the note is the main element.
- It looks like a real screenshot, not a designed text card.

## Not to confuse with

- **Post-it:** handwriting on paper, not a screen.
- **Listicle:** a list in a designed layout; a list typed in a phone note is Notes app.
- **Letter:** a signed note on paper.
- **Web search:** a search bar or results.

## Quick test

Does it look like a screenshot of a phone note, app bar and all?

## Does not belong in the format

- The list structure or the tips: structure or Angle. The phone note is the carrier, so it decides the format.

## Variants

- **Shopping list:** a list of items to buy, one of them the product. Tag it as Notes app.
- **To-do list:** tasks, some ticked off. Tag it as Notes app.

## Examples

- ✅ A note titled "things that actually helped my sleep", four lines, the last one the pillow spray.
- ✅ A shopping list: "oat milk, eggs, the protein bar everyone keeps talking about".
- ❌ The same list on a yellow sticky note on the fridge: Post-it.
