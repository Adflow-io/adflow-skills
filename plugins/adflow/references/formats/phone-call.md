# Phone call

A recorded phone call carries the ad: the phone or call screen is visible and both sides are heard.

Family: Video, person to camera.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A call is visible as a call: the call screen on a phone, a phone on speaker on a table, or a person on the phone.
- The call audio is heard, with two voices; one side can be off screen.
- The conversation on the call carries the message.

## Not to confuse with

- **Talking head:** one person talks to the camera, not into a call.
- **Skit:** an acted scene in person; an acted call is a Phone call only if the call itself is the frame.

## Quick test

Is a phone call visible on screen and are two voices heard on it? Check speech and what is on screen in the
per-second analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- A real customer or a staged call, recorded or reenacted: production.
- Customer service, a thank-you, a complaint fixed: the message, Angle.

## Variants

- **Calling customers:** the brand calls a buyer and asks how it went. Tag it as Phone call.
- **Calling a friend or parent:** a person calls someone close about the product. Tag it as Phone call.

## Examples

- ✅ The owner of a bakery calls a customer on speaker to ask how the birthday cake went.
- ✅ A woman calls her mother to tell her the hearing aid she ordered fits perfectly.
- ❌ The bakery owner telling the camera about her happiest customer: Talking head.
