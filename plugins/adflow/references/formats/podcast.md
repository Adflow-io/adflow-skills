# Podcast

Two or more people talk to each other at microphones on a podcast set, played as a clip from an episode.

Family: Video, person to camera.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A podcast setup is visible: microphones on arms or stands, often headphones, a set, desk or couch.
- Two or more people talk with each other, not straight to the viewer.
- It plays as a cut from a conversation; the product comes up in the talk.

## Not to confuse with

- **Talking head:** one person talks to you. One person alone at a podcast mic, speaking to camera, is a Talking head.
- **Skit:** an acted scene with roles, not a conversation at microphones.

## Quick test

Does it look like a clip from a podcast episode, with microphones and at least two people? Confirm two voices in the
per-second analysis in Adflow, not from the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Who sits at the mics (founder, expert, host, creator): Creator.
- A real sponsored show or a set built for the ad: production, a tag.
- An interview about results or a debate: the message, Angle.

## Variants

- **Podcast clip:** a cut from a longer real episode, often with burned-in captions. Tag it as Podcast.
- **Host and guest:** a host asks, the guest (often an expert) answers. Tag it as Podcast.

## Examples

- ✅ Two hosts on a couch with microphones joke about the sleep tracker one of them wears.
- ✅ A nutritionist as a guest at a podcast desk explains why she switched protein powders.
- ❌ The same nutritionist alone in her kitchen, talking to the camera: Talking head.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=podcast` (2 videos), and its notes in
