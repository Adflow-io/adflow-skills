# Post-it

The message as a short handwritten note on a sticky note or scrap of paper, placed on a product, surface or photo.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A sticky note, torn paper or pen scrawl is visible on the product, a surface or a photo.
- The text looks handwritten.
- It is short: one thought, a line or two.

## Not to confuse with

- **Letter:** longer text, paragraphs, signed.
- **Notes app:** a note on a phone screen.
- **Sign:** a person holds up the message in a scene.

## Quick test

Does it look like someone jotted one quick thing on a sticky note?

## Does not belong in the format

- A reminder, tip or confession as the message: Angle.
- Real handwriting or a handwriting font: production, a tag.

## Variants

- **Notes on a fridge:** several sticky notes on a fridge or mirror. Tag it as Post-it.

## Examples

- ✅ A yellow sticky note on a bathroom mirror, "SPF. Every day. Even in winter.", next to the bottle.
- ✅ A torn paper note taped to a lunchbox: "Ate it all. Again."
- ❌ The same line in a phone note with the app bar visible: Notes app.
