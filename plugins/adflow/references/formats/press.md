# Press

The ad looks like media coverage: an article, a headline from a publication, or a row of press logos.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A publication look is visible: a masthead, an article layout, an editorial headline, or press or award logos.
- An outside publication (real or styled as one) appears to do the talking.
- The product is the subject of the coverage or sits next to it.

## Not to confuse with

- **Quote card:** a customer's quote, not a publication's.
- **Announcement:** a notice or flyer, not news coverage.
- **Letter:** a personal note, not an article.

## Quick test

Is an outside publication (its logo, layout or headline) doing the talking?

## Does not belong in the format

- Earned media or "as seen in" as proof: Angle (social proof).
- An advertorial landing page: the landing page, not the ad's format.

## Variants

- **Breaking news:** a news-alert look with a banner or "breaking" tag. Tag it as Press.
- **Advertorial:** looks like an article page, headline and photo. Tag it as Press.

## Examples

- ✅ A mock newspaper column: "Parents are switching to the stroller that folds with one hand".
- ✅ A row of five magazine logos above a packshot with "As seen in", for a trust angle.
- ❌ A customer's five-star review set large in quote marks: Quote card.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, `vf=press` (14 examples) and `vf=news` (1); notes:

  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

Adcrate is positive; Dara Denney rates it tier F. It depends on the brand and on having coverage to show.
