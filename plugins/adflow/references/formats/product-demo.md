# Product demo

The product is shown being used, poured, applied, worn or tested, and that use carries the video.

Family: Video, product on screen.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The product in use is on screen for most of the video.
- A voice-over, text, or the product's own sound may explain it; no person tells a story to camera for most of it.
- The camera watches the use from the side or front, not through the user's eyes.

## Not to confuse with

- **POV:** the same use filmed first-person, hands in frame.
- **Unboxing / Try-on / First-try reaction:** opening a package, showing how it looks on, or a first reaction.
- **Behind the scenes:** making or packing the product, not using it.
- **B-roll with voice-over / B-roll with text:** when a voice or text carries the story over changing shots (some of
  them pouring or using the product), it is B-roll. It is Product demo only when the use itself is the story.

## Quick test

Is the product being used or tested on screen for most of the video? Check what is on screen in the per-second
analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Proof, mechanism, "how it works": the message, Angle.
- UGC, studio, AI generated: production, a tag. A dense, gag-heavy edit is an editing choice; the library's
  `high-production-edit` is not a card.

## Variants

- **How-to:** steps shown in order. Tag it as Product demo.
- **Stress test:** a problem is caused on camera and the product handles it. Tag it as Product demo.
- **Multitasking demo:** the product used during another task. Tag it as Product demo.
- **ASMR:** close-ups with amplified product sounds, little or no voice. Tag it as Product demo.

## Examples

- ✅ A hand spills red wine on a sofa cover and wipes it off in one move.
- ✅ Close-ups of a coffee grinder with the crunch of the beans as the only sound.
- ❌ The same wine spill filmed from the user's eyes, hands in frame: POV.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=live-demo` (1 video) and `vf=asmr` (6; three also tagged
  unboxing, which belong under Unboxing here), and its notes in [formats/live-demo](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/live-demo) and
  [formats/asmr](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/asmr).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

The gallery (from its sources) calls it the classic; leaning only on it causes fatigue. Not a rule for every brand.
