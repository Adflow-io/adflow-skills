# Product hero

One product shown large on a plain, coloured or studio background, with a short headline or no text at all.

Family: Static, image-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- One product (or one pack) fills most of the frame and is the first thing you see.
- The background is plain, coloured or studio, not a real-life scene.
- Text is short: a headline, one claim, a logo, or nothing.

## Not to confuse with

- **Lifestyle photo:** the product sits in a real setting or is in use.
- **Headline + benefits:** a headline plus a block of benefits carries the ad; the product supports it.
- **Feature callouts:** lines or arrows run from the product to labelled parts.

## Quick test

Cover the text: is what is left one product on a clean background?

## Does not belong in the format

- A discount banner on the packshot: Offer (the library's `offer-banner` is an offer, not a format).
- Studio shoot, 3D render or AI packshot: production, a tag.
- A catalogue or carousel of packshots: the ad type in Adflow.

## Variants

- **Packshot on colour:** the product cut out on a brand colour, no text or a logo only. Tag it as Product hero.
- **Product with one claim:** the packshot plus one short line. Tag it as Product hero.

## Examples

- ✅ A serum bottle centred on a soft peach background with the line "Glow by Friday".
- ✅ A trail-running shoe on light grey with "Now in wide fit", for a launch angle.
- ❌ The same serum on a bathroom shelf between towels and plants: Lifestyle photo.
- Real ads: no examples in the library yet.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
