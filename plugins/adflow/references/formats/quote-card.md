# Quote card

A customer quote or review designed as the main visual, often with stars and a name.

Family: Static, native screenshot.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A customer quote or review is the largest element.
- It is designed (brand fonts, quote marks, stars), not a screenshot with app chrome.
- A name, initials or "verified buyer" line often sits under it; the product is small or absent.

## Not to confuse with

- **Comment screenshot:** the review as a real app screenshot.
- **Press:** a quote from a publication.
- **Letter:** a long, signed personal note.
- **Grid:** tiles of photos; several review cards together stay a Quote card.

## Quick test

Is a designed customer quote the biggest thing, without app chrome?

## Does not belong in the format

- Testimonial or review as the message: Angle (social proof). The library's `testimonial` (a person telling their whole
  story on video) is that message, not a format.

## Variants

- **Star rating card:** stars plus a short quote. Tag it as Quote card.
- **Review grid:** several review cards together. Tag it as Quote card.

## Examples

- ✅ "I've bought four for my sisters" in big quote marks, five stars, "Anna, verified buyer", a small packshot.
- ✅ Three review cards about the shoes still looking new after a year, for a durability angle.
- ❌ A screenshot of the same review in the shop's app, avatar and like count included: Comment screenshot.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=review` (5 examples), and its notes in
