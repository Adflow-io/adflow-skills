# Receipt

An itemised slip or order overview is the hero of the ad: a paper receipt, a ticket or coupon, or a shopping cart
screen, with the products or the deal printed on it.

Family: Static, native screenshot.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The main element looks like a real receipt, ticket, coupon or cart: a white slip or a checkout screen.
- Slip or cart details are visible: line items, prices, totals, a barcode, tear lines or a "checkout" button.
- The products or the deal are written on that slip or screen, not around it.

## Not to confuse with

- **Comparison table:** two options side by side in rows or columns; a receipt lists one order.
- **Notes app:** a phone note with a typed list, without prices or totals.
- **Product hero:** the product carries the ad; a receipt may show products, but the slip is the hero.
- **Announcement:** a notice or flyer look without line items.

## Quick test

Cover the products: does what is left still look like a receipt, ticket or cart with lines and a total?

## Does not belong in the format

- The discount itself: Offer. A receipt can show a deal or no deal at all.
- "Retargeting" or "proof" in the name: that is the funnel or the message (Awareness, Angle), not the form.
- What the receipt argues (value for money, bundle, what you get): Angle.

## Variants

- **Paper receipt:** a printed slip with line items and a total. Tag it as Receipt.
- **Ticket or coupon:** a slip with tear lines or a barcode and one deal printed on it. Tag it as Receipt.
- **Cart screenshot:** an online cart or checkout page with line items and totals. Tag it as Receipt.

## Examples

- ✅ A tea brand shows a paper receipt listing its starter set item by item, with the total at the bottom.
- ✅ A skincare brand shows a checkout screen with three products, the crossed-out price and the free gift line.
- ❌ A phone note with "my morning routine: 1. … 2. … 3. …" and no prices: Notes app.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=receipt` (2 examples) and `vf=cart-screenshot` (2), and its
  notes in [formats/receipt](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/receipt) and
  [formats/cart-screenshot](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/cart-screenshot).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

The swipe library uses it mostly around sales and bundles, where seeing the order and the total makes the value
concrete. Only a few examples, so test before leaning on it.
