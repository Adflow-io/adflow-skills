# Sign

A person or a place holds up a physical sign, often handwritten, that carries the message.

Family: Static, image-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A physical sign (cardboard, paper, a board) or writing on the body is visible.
- The message is written on that sign.
- It is photographed in a real scene, usually held by a person.

## Not to confuse with

- **Post-it:** a small sticky note on a product or surface, nobody holding it up.
- **Ad in the wild:** a printed, paid ad placement such as a billboard.
- **Announcement:** a designed notice or flyer.

## Quick test

Is someone (or something) holding up the message on a physical sign?

## Does not belong in the format

- The person holding the sign: Creator.
- A protest, stunt or confession idea: Angle.

## Variants

- **Written on body:** the message is written on a hand, arm or back. Tag it as Sign.

## Examples

- ✅ A woman on a street corner with a cardboard sign "I stopped hitting snooze", the product at her feet.
- ✅ A shop window with a chalkboard sign "Yes, the refill is cheaper", for a price objection.
- ❌ A sticky note on a jar lid reading "don't eat it all": Post-it.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=sign` (4 examples: 2 static, 2 video), and its notes in
