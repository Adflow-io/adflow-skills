# Split screen

The image is cut into two halves side by side (or top and bottom), each showing something different.

Family: Static, image-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- Exactly two panels, clearly separated by a line, a colour change or a hard edge.
- Each half is its own image or designed panel.
- The two halves show different subjects, for example the product and an alternative, or a pitch and its proof.

## Not to confuse with

- **Before / after:** the same subject in two states, marked as before and after.
- **Comparison table:** rows and columns of criteria, not two pictures.
- **Grid:** three or more tiles.

## Quick test

Is the picture cut into exactly two halves that show different things?

## Does not belong in the format

- "Us vs them" or "this vs that": the message, Angle. Any message fits two halves.
- Naming a competitor or not: an Angle decision, not a format.

## Variants

- **Us vs them in two pictures:** the product on one side, the alternative on the other. Tag it as Split screen.

## Examples

- ✅ Left: a knot of charging cables on a desk; right: one tidy charging dock.
- ✅ Left: the product box with its logo; right: a close-up of the cream's texture on skin, for a sensorial angle.
