# Statistic

One big number is the eye-catcher, usually over a product or lifestyle photo, with small supporting text.

Family: Static, text-led.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- One number (or a few) is clearly the largest element in the ad.
- The supporting text is small and explains the number.
- Take the number away and the ad loses its point.

## Not to confuse with

- **Headline + benefits:** a text headline leads, no dominant number.
- **Diagram:** a chart that helps the viewer choose.
- **Quote card:** a customer quote is the main element; stars are not a statistic.

## Quick test

Cover the image: is what you remember a number?

## Does not belong in the format

- What the number means (a result, units sold, a ranking): Angle.
- A discount number ("40% OFF"): that is the Offer, not a statistic. Tag the form it sits in: Product hero when
  the product carries the ad, Announcement when it looks like a notice or banner.
- Whether the number is from a study: copy and proof, not the format.

## Variants

- **Line chart:** a line or curve shows the change over time or use. Tag it as Statistic.
- **Case study result:** several result numbers, each with a small image, and a study footnote. Tag it as Statistic.

## Examples

- ✅ "92%" huge over a close-up of hair, with "felt softer after one wash" small underneath.
- ✅ "1,000,000 dogs fed" across a photo of a dog bowl, for a trust angle.
- ❌ A "Which roast is yours?" chart with arrows to three coffees: Diagram.
