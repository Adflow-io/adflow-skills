# Street interview

People in a public place are stopped and answer a question or try the product on camera.

Family: Video, person to camera.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A public setting: street, park, shop, market, event.
- Several people who look like passers-by answer on camera, one after another or in a small group.
- An interviewer asks the questions, often off screen or only a hand with a microphone.

## Not to confuse with

- **Talking head:** one planned speaker, or a planned interview in a set-up place.
- **First-try reaction:** one person tries the product at home or in a studio; strangers in public trying it are a
  Street interview.

## Quick test

Are strangers in a public place stopped and asked something on camera? Check the setting and who speaks in the
per-second analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Real passers-by or paid actors: production, a tag.
- A quiz, dare or contest with a rule: a concept or Angle; the library's `challenge` label is not a card.
- Social proof, "what do people think": the message, Angle.

## Variants

- **Blind test with strangers:** passers-by try two unlabelled products and pick one. Tag it as Street interview.
- **Quiz:** passers-by guess a price, an ingredient or a fact. Tag it as Street interview.

## Examples

- ✅ A host with a microphone asks shoppers to guess what a reusable water bottle costs, then reveals the price.
- ✅ Students on a campus try two chocolate bars blind and say which one they would buy.
- ❌ A creator at her kitchen table tries the same chocolate bar for the first time: First-try reaction.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=street-interview` (3 videos), and its notes in
  [formats/street-interview](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/street-interview).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.
