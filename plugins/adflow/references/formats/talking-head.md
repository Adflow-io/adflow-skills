# Talking head

One person speaks to the camera for most of the video; their face and voice carry the ad.

Family: Video, person to camera.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- One person is on screen and talks to the camera for most of the runtime.
- Their own voice carries the message; cutaways support it and never take over.
- No more specific person card fits (comment reaction, green screen, whiteboard, podcast, street interview).

## Not to confuse with

- **Comment reaction:** the video opens on a comment on screen that the person then answers.
- **Green screen:** the person stands in front of a full-frame screenshot, page or video.
- **First-try reaction:** the person tries the product on camera and the reaction is the point.
- **B-roll with voice-over:** the voice goes on, but the speaker is off screen for most of the video.

## Quick test

Is one person's face on screen, speaking to you, for more than half the video? Decide it from the per-second analysis
in Adflow (what is on screen, text on screen, speech versus song lyrics), never from the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Founder, employee, expert, creator: the sender, so Creator. A founder to camera is a talking head with Creator =
  founder. Founder and UGC are not formats; the library's `founder`, `egc` and `ugc` labels are not cards.
- UGC, selfie (front camera at arm's length), studio, AI avatar: production, a tag. Selfie is a production note, not
  a variant. Testimonial, review, myth-busting: the message, Angle; the library's `testimonial` is not a card.
- Several creators cut together, or a live-shopping look: an edit or production choice. The library's `ugc-mashup`
  and `live-selling` are not cards; tag the form each clip shows.

## Variants

- **Yapper:** fast, raw, little editing. Tag it as Talking head.
- **Graphic yap:** graphics pop up around the speaker in the same shot. Tag it as Talking head.
- **Explainer:** the speaker teaches something, often with props on the table. Tag it as Talking head.
- **Q&A or walk and talk:** a planned off-screen interviewer, or the speaker walking. Tag it as Talking head.

## Examples

- ✅ A creator in her bathroom explains in 30 seconds why she dropped three steps from her skincare routine.
- ✅ The founder of a coffee brand at his desk explains why the beans cost more (Creator = founder).
- ❌ A coach in front of a full-frame screenshot of a study she points at: Green screen.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=yapper` (14 videos), `vf=graphic-yap` (5), `vf=explainer` (2)
  and `vf=interview` (1), and its notes in [formats/yapper](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/yapper), [formats/graphic-yap](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/graphic-yap),
  [formats/explainer](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/explainer) and [formats/interview](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/interview).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

Dara Denney's tier list (about 20,000 ads) puts the yapper in tier A. Not a rule for every brand.
