# Text message

A chat or message thread in the look of a messaging app, read as if over someone's shoulder.

Family: Static, native screenshot.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- Chat bubbles in a messaging layout, sender and receiver on opposite sides.
- One or more messages, often a short back-and-forth.
- The app's chrome (header, name, input bar) is often visible.

## Not to confuse with

- **Comment screenshot:** a public comment under a post.
- **Comment response:** a public comment answered by the brand.
- **Notes app:** one person's note, no bubbles.

## Quick test

Does it look like you are reading someone's chat?

## Does not belong in the format

- A friend's recommendation or a parent's nagging: Angle.
- Who is "in" the chat: copy, not Creator, unless a real creator is shown.

## Variants

- **Chat app look:** styled after one specific messaging app, for example WhatsApp or iMessage. Tag it as Text message.
- **Group chat:** several senders in one thread. Tag it as Text message.

## Examples

- ✅ "where did you get that lamp??" answered with "€40, I swear" and a photo of the lamp.
- ✅ A parent texting "did you take your vitamins", the reply a photo of the gummy jar.
- ❌ The same question posted under a video and answered by the brand: Comment response.
