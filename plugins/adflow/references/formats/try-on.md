# Try-on

A person puts the product on and shows how it looks on them.

Family: Video, product on screen.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A person puts the product on, on camera: clothing, shoes, jewellery, glasses, a bag, a watch.
- The result on the body is shown: in a mirror, turning, close-ups, or several pieces one after another.

## Not to confuse with

- **Product demo:** the product is used for what it does, not shown for how it looks on.
- **Unboxing:** the opening of the package is the point, not wearing it.
- **Vlog:** a get-ready-with-me follows a whole routine; a try-on stays on the product being put on and shown.
- **POV:** filmed first-person, it is still Try-on when putting the product on and showing it is the point.

## Quick test

Does someone put the product on and show how it looks on them for most of the video? Check what is on screen in the
per-second analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- UGC, mirror selfie, studio model: production, a tag.
- Who wears it (creator, founder, customer): Creator. Fit, size, "looks expensive": the message, Angle.

## Variants

- **Styling:** one piece combined into several outfits. Tag it as Try-on.
- **Stacking:** rings, bracelets or necklaces added one by one. Tag it as Try-on.
- **Haul:** several items tried on in a row. Tag it as Try-on.

## Examples

- ✅ A woman puts on a linen dress, turns in front of the mirror and shows it with two pairs of shoes.
- ✅ A man stacks three bracelets on his wrist, one at a time, close to the camera.
- ❌ A hand holding the same bracelet up to the light, never worn: Product demo.
- Real ads: No examples in the library yet.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

The gallery (from its sources) rates it strong for fashion and jewellery. Not a rule for every brand.
