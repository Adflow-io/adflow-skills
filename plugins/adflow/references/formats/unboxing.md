# Unboxing

A package is opened on screen and the product is taken out and shown for the first time.

Family: Video, product on screen.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A package (box, mailer, pouch, gift wrap) is opened on camera.
- The product is taken out and shown; the reveal is the spine of the video.

## Not to confuse with

- **Product demo:** the product is already out and in use.
- **Try-on:** the point is how it looks on, not what came in the box.
- **First-try reaction:** the point is the reaction to the first taste or use, not the opening.
- **POV:** filmed first-person, it is still Unboxing when opening the package is the point.

## Quick test

Is the video built around opening a package and showing what is inside? Check what is on screen in the per-second
analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Gift, gift set, "what you get for the price": the message or the offer, Angle or Offer.
- Who opens it (customer, creator, founder): Creator. Sound-led filming: production.

## Variants

- **What's in the box:** every part laid out after opening. Tag it as Unboxing.
- **Gift reveal:** someone opens it as a present. Tag it as Unboxing.
- **Sound-led unboxing:** amplified tape, paper and click sounds. Tag it as Unboxing.

## Examples

- ✅ A creator cuts open a mailer and lays out the five parts of a skincare starter set.
- ✅ A child unwraps a building kit as a birthday present and holds up the pieces.
- ❌ The same skincare set already open, a hand applying the serum: Product demo.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=unboxing` (4 videos, three of them also tagged `asmr`), and
  its notes in [formats/unboxing](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/unboxing).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

The gallery (from its sources) rates it strong for gifts and sets. Not a rule for every brand.
