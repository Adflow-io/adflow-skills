# Vlog

A handheld camera follows a person through a stretch of their day, the product part of it.

Family: Video, footage with voice or text.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- The camera follows one person through several moments or places of their day or routine.
- The look is handheld or casual; the person is seen and often talks to camera in parts.
- The product shows up inside the routine, not as the only subject.

## Not to confuse with

- **Talking head:** one place, the person mostly speaking to camera.
- **B-roll with voice-over:** mixed footage that does not follow one person through their day.
- **Skit:** acted scenes with roles.

## Quick test

Does the video follow one person through several moments of their day? Check the scenes in the per-second analysis
in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Selfie, UGC, staged or real: production, a tag.
- Lifestyle, routine, "this is how I use it": the message, Angle.

## Variants

- **Day in the life:** morning to evening in short clips. Tag it as Vlog.
- **Get ready with me:** the morning or evening routine before going out. Tag it as Vlog.
- **Staged moment:** a set-up scene that looks caught on camera. Tag it as Vlog.
- **Device camera:** seen through a doorbell, dashcam or baby monitor (found footage). Tag it as Vlog.

## Examples

- ✅ A nurse films her night shift: the drive in, the break room, the compression socks she swears by.
- ✅ A doorbell camera shows a courier, then a dog greeting its owner with a new chew toy.
- ❌ The same nurse sitting in her car talking to camera about the socks: Talking head.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=found-footage` (2 videos, both with a second tag), and its
  notes in [formats/found-footage](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/found-footage).
  Library: <https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/files>.

## When it works

General signal from the sources, not a verdict for a brand. For a brand, its own performance in Adflow decides:
spend first, then ROAS, per creative family.

Dara Denney's tier list puts it at tier D: it scales poorly on paid. Not a rule for every brand.
