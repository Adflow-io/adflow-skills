# Whiteboard

The message is written or drawn in marker on a real whiteboard or flip chart while someone explains it.

Family: Video, person to camera.
Back to the [gallery](../format-gallery.md) · rules in [format-classification.md](../format-classification.md).

## Must be true

- A real whiteboard or flip chart is the main visual for most of the video.
- The message is handwritten or drawn on it in marker: words, arrows, a crossed-out option, a simple chart.
- Someone explains it, on camera next to the board or as a voice while a hand writes.

## Not to confuse with

- **Talking head:** the person talks without a board, or the board is only a prop behind them.
- **Green screen:** a digital screen behind the person, not a board with marker.
- **Animation:** drawings made digitally and filling the frame, not marker on a real board.

## Quick test

Is a real board with marker writing the thing you look at for most of the video? Check what is on screen in the
per-second analysis in Adflow, not the thumbnail or the ad name.

## Does not belong in the format

- Mechanism, comparison, "how it works": the message, Angle.
- Who holds the marker (founder, expert, creator): Creator.

## Variants

- **Flip chart:** paper pad on a stand, pages flipped per point. Tag it as Whiteboard.
- **Board only:** a hand writes while a voice explains, no face. Tag it as Whiteboard.

## Examples

- ✅ A dentist draws a tooth on a whiteboard and shows where a regular toothbrush misses plaque.
- ✅ A founder writes three prices on a flip chart and crosses out two to show what a meal kit saves.
- ❌ The dentist talking to camera with an unused whiteboard in the background: Talking head.
- Real ads: the visual-formats library, files named `vf=whiteboard` (7 videos; the slug is shared with statics), and
  its notes in [formats/whiteboard](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/visual-formats/tree/main/formats/whiteboard).
