# Organic research: viral Instagram videos as ad ideas

How to find viral organic videos in a brand's niche and turn them into ad ideas. Remaking a video whose idea already
proved itself is one of the most reliable ways to find winning ads. Source: the team handbook
[Virale Instagram video's vinden voor ad-ideeën](https://app.notion.com/p/adflow/Virale-Instagram-video-s-vinden-voor-ad-idee-n-421e5be75c8081f68720e6d37d5374f8) (Notion). Share that link with the person who does
steps 2 and 3; it has screenshots.

Who does what: Claude makes the keywords and works with the videos once they are in Adflow. The searching and
downloading is done by a person, on a phone.

## 1. Keywords (Claude)
Make 20 keywords of at most two words in the brand's niche, tied to the mass desires of the audience (more than one
desire). Mark the ones closest to the brand. Add English keywords too; English reels reach far bigger numbers.

## 2. Search Instagram (person, on a phone)
Instagram search for reels only works in the app, not on desktop. Search icon → keyword → tab Reels.

Keep a video when it:
- went viral: roughly 100K+ views for Dutch, 1M+ for English
- has many views compared to followers (views ÷ followers): the higher, the more the topic carried it, not the creator
- is easy to remake with existing footage or a creator, and easy to brief
- lets the product fit in naturally as an ad

For each video, note why it went viral: the comments (discussion, tagging, questions), the topic and message, and
what the viewer gets out of it. Aim for at least 5 videos per keyword and more than 10 in total.

## 3. Save in Adflow (person)
Download the video (the [handbook](https://app.notion.com/p/adflow/Virale-Instagram-video-s-vinden-voor-ad-idee-n-421e5be75c8081f68720e6d37d5374f8) names a tool) and upload it to the brand's **Inspiratie** board in Adflow. Adflow
stores a transcript and description per video, so Claude can read it afterwards.

## 4. Pick the best (person, Claude can help)
Label each video **Easy** (quick to make) and **High chance** (likely to work). Videos with both labels go into the
next sprint. Per video answer: how do we adapt it to the brand, how do we make it better, why did it go viral, and how
do we make it simple enough to finish within a week?

## 5. Use it (Claude)
With the videos in the Inspiratie board, Claude can, per video name: explain why it went viral and what the brand can
learn, write hooks in its style, make a concept or a creator brief to remake it, and say which videos fit the brand's
personas and angles best or are easiest to remake with existing footage.

**Watch out:** not every viral video is a good ad. If the product does not fit the story naturally, skip it. That is
where most remakes go wrong.
